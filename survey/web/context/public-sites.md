# Public practice sites

These tests drive public third-party websites, mostly sites built for
practising test automation. They are not under our control and can change.

- Dismiss cookie banners by rejecting non-essential cookies, and close ads,
  newsletter popups and overlays that cover the page, without reporting them
  as failures.
- Never submit a payment, place a real order or send a message to a real
  person. When a step reaches a payment form, stop there.
- When a page says a link opens in a new tab, switch to that tab before acting
  on it.
