Accounts

A MUI DataGrid, measured on https://mui.com/x/react-data-grid/ (2026-09-23): nesting, class names, roles and aria wiring as the component renders them. There is no <table> here — the structure is carried entirely by ARIA. role="grid" with aria-rowcount="9" and aria-colcount="7" sits on .MuiDataGrid-main, two divs inside the #accounts-grid wrapper the author names, under containers marked role="none" and role="presentation". Rows are role="row": the header is aria-rowindex="1" and the eight accounts are 2…9. Cells are role="columnheader" and role="gridcell", ordered by aria-colindex — 1 is the select-all checkbox column, whose heading is blank, then Account, Number, Balance, Status, Owner and Actions at 2…7. Every row begins with a role="none" filler and ends with another; they are not cells and take no column. A read of Account, Balance, Status and Owner by heading should return the 8 accounts, with the checkbox column's empty heading and the fillers skipped. Two accounts belong to Sarah Mitchell, so a step that names an owner is ambiguous and _row is what tells them apart. The Status filter hides the rows that do not match with display:none and does not renumber anything: filter to Frozen and the two visible rows still read aria-rowindex="5" and "9", which is the short version of why _row counts data rows and aria-rowindex is not it.

Trades

An ag-Grid whose Symbol column is pinned left. This markup is documented, not measured: both ag-grid.com/example/ and its column-pinning docs page were walked headless on 2026-09-23 and neither rendered a single .ag-row within 90 seconds, so the nesting follows ag-Grid's documented DOM for v30+. The point of the fixture is what pinning does to a row: every row exists twice. .ag-pinned-left-cols-container holds a role="row" with the one Symbol cell at aria-colindex="1", and .ag-center-cols-container holds another role="row", for the same trade, with Side, Quantity, Price, Value and Status at aria-colindex 2…6. The header is split the same way, both halves marked aria-rowindex="1". The only thing joining the two fragments of a trade is that they carry the same row-index, row-id and aria-rowindex — the CSS puts them side by side, and on screen there is no seam to see. A read should return 6 trades, each with its Symbol taken from the pinned fragment and the rest from the centre one; a reader that walks only one container gets six half-records that look complete. Show no trades empties the grid the way the component does — no row at all, just a <div class="ag-overlay-no-rows-center">No Rows To Show</div>, which is not a row and not a colspan placeholder either, so an empty read has to recognise a third shape.
